My approach

Urdu type with intention

01

Nastaliq as the primary language

The script is treated with respect, preserving its natural diagonal movement and the tactility of its joining forms.

02

Line breaks follow the verse

Each line is positioned to support rhythm, breath, and poetic pacing, rather than being forced into rigid container logic.

03

Legibility over ornament

The composition supports the verse; typography, scale, contrast, and spacing frame the poem rather than compete with it.

About

Designing poetry into form.

I am a designer interested in Urdu typography, visual storytelling, and the relationship between poetry and page composition. My work explores how a verse can acquire a stronger visual identity without losing its sincerity, tone, or reading flow.

The portfolio focuses on self-initiated poster designs and typographic experiments that explore language, rhythm, and style.

“Because a good verse deserves better than a default font.”
